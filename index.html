Privacy & methods

All matching happens in your browser; this file does not send your answers to any service. This is an educational heuristic, not a validated political-psychology instrument. Profile coordinates are editorial approximations rather than universal definitions; comparison scores are for exploration and not proof of ideological commitment.

Highly specific or extremist ideologies require beliefs that these questions do not measure. Those are viewable in the encyclopedia but not automatically assigned as your closest ideology.
